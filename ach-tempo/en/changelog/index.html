Changelog

Notable changes by version.

This project follows Keep a Changelog and Semantic Versioning.

1.0.0 — 2026-06-27

Added

  • World reaction (Achieve.Tempo.World): WorldReaction spreading fire/water/ice/electricity by rules on a flat grid, per-cell ElementalState, MaterialDef, table-driven ReactionRule (single-field ; input), and the live-event ReactionEvent.
  • Director (Achieve.Tempo.Director): DirectorBrain with a default policy extended via virtual, the SeedAction seeding action plus four built-ins (DryUpwind/IgniteUpwind/ChargeStorm/SummonRain), and PhaseFsm phase switching (hysteresis).
  • Tension (Achieve.Tempo.Tension): TensionModel combining two signals into 0–1 (rise fast, fall slow).
  • Config (Achieve.Tempo.Config): the code-separated data center DataService<T> and TensionConfig/PhaseData/ActionData/RuleData (with defaults).
  • Editor tools (Achieve.Tempo.Editor): RuleValidator (pre-build rule-table check) and DebugWindow (live tension/phase view).
  • Tests: EditMode (TensionModel/PhaseFsm/ReactionRule/RuleValidator), PlayMode (spread direction, quench, event cleanup).
  • Sample: Samples~/BasicGrassFire — the simplest single loop.
  • Docs: fumadocs site (ko/en/ja/zh).
